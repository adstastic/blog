Quoting [Neal Stephenson](https://nealstephenson.substack.com/p/emerson-ai-and-the-force):

> Now the cycle of enthusiasm and disillusionment is repeating itself with AI. This time, though, it’s happening a lot faster, because we all have a kind of preloaded cynicism about what new technology can and can’t do for us.

> the system we’ve traditionally used for evaluating students’ performance - homework and tests - just happens to be exquisitely vulnerable to being hacked by students who simply use conversational AI systems to do all the work for them.

> The only way to fairly evaluate how much a student has learned now is by marching them into a classroom with no electronics, handing them a pencil and a blank blue book, and assigning them an essay to write or a math problem to solve.
