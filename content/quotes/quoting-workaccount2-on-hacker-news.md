Quoting [Simon Willison&#39;s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/18/context-rot/#atom-everything):

> **context rot**, where as context grows and especially if it grows with lots of distractions and dead ends, the output quality falls off rapidly. Even with good context the rot will start to become apparent around 100k tokens (with Gemini 2.5).

They really need to figure out a way to delete or &#34;forget&#34; prior context, so the user or even the model can go back and prune poisonous tokens.
